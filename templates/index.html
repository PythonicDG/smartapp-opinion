Help & Support

Need assistance using SMARTVIEW? Here are quick answers to the most common questions to get you up and running fast.

How do I search an app?

Use the search bar to start typing an app name. SMARTVIEW will auto-suggest live Google Play apps with icons and developer details.

Are reviews real-time?

Yes. Reviews are fetched live from Google Play every time you run an analysis. No cached or outdated data is used.

How accurate is sentiment analysis?

SMARTVIEW uses an optimized LSTM deep-learning model trained on real app reviews, delivering high-accuracy sentiment predictions.
